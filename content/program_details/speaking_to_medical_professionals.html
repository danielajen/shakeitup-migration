Speaking to Medical Professionals

The goal of this workshop is to learn strategies that improve communication with health care professionals.

Objectives

  1. Learn assertiveness training techniques to advocate for proper medical care and equipment
  2. Practice self advocacy techniques and share experiences and lessons learned

This is a continuation of self advocacy skills training that focuses specifically on interactions with medical and rehabilitation professionals. Begin by using applicable the scenarios from the earlier session as a basis for discussion. Participants are encouraged to provide examples from their own life experiences. Then the group is called upon to problem-solve and role-play possible responses to each example.
