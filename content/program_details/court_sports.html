Court Sports

The goal of court sports is to introduce the group to a variety of wheelchair sports that may be played in an outdoor/indoor court.

Objectives

  1. Participant will be introduced to the rules of volleyball, wheelchair rugby and wheelchair football.
  2. Participant will experience playing a variety of group court sports.
  3. Participant will receive information about joining local basketball and rugby teams.

Plan

  1. Coordinate court time with school or community gym.
  2. Staff needed to referee, retrieve balls, set-up goals.

Staff

Four staff for group of twelve.

Site criteria

Accessible indoor/outdoor court; shaded area if outdoors, accessible bathrooms.

Supplies and equipment

Football
Volleyball
Cones
Whistles
First aid kit
Water bottle
